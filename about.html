About Elevartemis

We like businesses with big ideas.

  • The local business that has been someone's dream for ten years.
  • The contractor who knows half his customers by name.
  • The shop owner who built something from nothing.
  • The service business that's grown faster than its marketing.
  • The entrepreneur who knows they need marketing—but doesn't have the time, budget, or desire to build an entire marketing department.

Those are our people.

Elevartemis exists to help small businesses do smart, purposeful marketing without making marketing their second full-time job.

Hi, I'm Robyn.

I'm the person behind Elevartemis.

I've worked inside businesses where marketing doesn't happen in a vacuum. It has to work alongside sales, operations, leadership, budgets, deadlines, customers, and everything else that keeps a business moving.

I've seen what happens when marketing is treated as an afterthought.

I've also seen what happens when someone finally steps back, looks at the whole picture, and asks:

What are we actually trying to accomplish?

That's the kind of marketing I believe in.

  • Not marketing for the sake of marketing.
  • Not more content just to have more content.
  • Not spending money because someone said you "should."

Marketing with a purpose.

We're building the kind of marketing company we'd want to hire.

That means a few things.

  1. 01

    Marketing should have a purpose.

    Pretty is nice. Clever is fun. But your marketing should ultimately help your business accomplish something.

  2. 02

    Small businesses deserve big-business thinking.

    You shouldn't need a six-person marketing department to have a thoughtful strategy.

  3. 03

    Good ideas don't have to be complicated.

    Sometimes the smartest solution is also the simplest one.

  4. 04

    Your marketing should sound like you.

    We don't believe every business needs to sound like the same polished corporation. Your personality is part of what makes your business worth choosing.

    "Leveraging synergies to deliver solutions.""We'll be there Tuesday. Coffee's on us."
  5. 05

    People do business with people.

    Technology can help you reach people. Relationships are what make them remember you.

  6. 06

    You shouldn't have to figure it all out yourself.

    That's where we come in.
